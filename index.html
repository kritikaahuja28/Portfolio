01 · About

About Me

I am a GenAI Engineer and Senior Associate 2 at Ernst & Young Global Delivery Services (EY-GDS) in Gurugram, India, with a strong passion for leveraging artificial intelligence to solve complex business challenges. I own the design of a finance chatbot used by EY teams worldwide, built on RAG, LangGraph and Elasticsearch. I have spent four years shipping ML and LLM systems to production, from OCR pipelines to text-to-SQL agents. My expertise spans Python, machine learning algorithms, and frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, OpenCV, Flask and FastAPI.

With a dual degree (BE in Computer Science + MBA) from Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology, I have developed a deep understanding of both technical and business aspects of AI solutions. Over the years, I have contributed to a wide range of projects, from developing Gen-AI solutions to integrating OpenAI APIs and automating document generation processes. I am passionate about driving innovation and improving operational efficiency through AI.

On the side, I built and run Agaah, a live news app on web and Android that turns stories from 37 Indian and world feeds into short summaries using open-source LLMs.

In addition to my professional work, I have been recognized for my contributions, including winning multiple hackathons such as the Smart India Hackathon 2020 and Bosch AI Hackathon 2021. I am also actively involved in extracurricular activities, including volunteering for community events like blood donation camps.

I am always eager to explore new AI trends and look forward to contributing to impactful projects that drive positive change.
